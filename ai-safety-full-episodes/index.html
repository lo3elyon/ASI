    Notes & method

    Method

    • Full episodes only. Every entry is a complete podcast episode, interview, debate, talk, panel or hearing recording. Clip compilations, reaction videos and news cut-downs were left out.
    • Verified against transcripts. Each episode was checked against its YouTube transcript, description or chapter list to confirm who actually speaks and what is covered. The evidence line in each episode's details quotes what the check rested on.
    • One copy per event. When several channels uploaded the same hearing or talk, only one upload is kept (usually the official or longest one).
    • Dates. Dates are upload dates. A date marked ~ is estimated because the page did not show an exact one.
    • Chapters. Topic timestamps come from descriptions or transcripts and can be a few seconds off. Topics without a time are listed but not seekable.
    • Window. October 1–27, 2026, compiled October 27, 2026. Eight full episodes from September 27–30 sit in "Just outside the window".

    Your data

    • Watch history, notes, favorites, queue, settings and achievements live in this browser's localStorage only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
    • Only playback time is counted: seeking, scrubbing and paused time never add to "watched". Percent watched is the share of the episode you actually played (in 10-second slices), so skipping ahead does not complete an episode.
    • An episode counts as completed at 90% coverage.
    • Use Export JSON on the History tab to back up or move your progress to another browser.

    Adding episodes

    New daily files go in data/daily/ and are listed in data/manifest.json. The app merges them on load, newest copy of an id wins. See the folder README.
