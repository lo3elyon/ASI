The story so far

A researcher quit Anthropic with a public warning, and then went on a media tour.

Why so many hour-long videos mention him.

Jacob Coxon is a former Anthropic researcher. On 8 September 2026 he resigned and posted a long warning about where AI is heading. The post went viral.

Over the following weeks he gave long interviews (The Megyn Kelly Show on 10 Sep, the PBD Podcast on 6 Oct), appeared in TV news segments, and in October testified at a New York City Council hearing on AI safeguards, on a whistleblower panel with Daniel Kokotajlo and Alex Turner.

His warning split people. Some podcasts treat him as a brave whistleblower; others call the timing a coordinated "doomer" campaign. This page collects both sides, but labels every video honestly: does he actually speak in it, and for how long?

Good to know

A few honest caveats.

The three TESTIMONY videos are the same event. AP, the NYC Council's own channel and The No Cap Press all streamed the same hearing. Watch one; the timestamps on each card tell you where he starts.
Searching inside YouTube? Auto-captions often spell his name "Coxin", so search for that too when jumping through a transcript.
Times are approximate. Timestamps and minutes of speaking come from transcripts and outside chapter listings, so expect them to be off by a minute or so. "1h+" means YouTube shows the video as over an hour but the exact length wasn't recorded.
One title is misleading. CNN-News18's upload names the Anthropic researcher in its title but only covers the end of the hearing; his own testimony isn't in that cut.
Claims are the speakers' own. Summaries report what was said (for example, PBD's "90% certain" spies claim, made without evidence). They are not checked as true.
