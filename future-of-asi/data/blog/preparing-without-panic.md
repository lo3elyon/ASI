When people hear "prepare for AI catastrophe" they imagine bunkers and canned food. That is not what I mean, and it is not what most researchers mean either.

## Most risks are about systems, not robots

The realistic near term shocks from AI look like large cyber attacks on banks or power, floods of convincing fake information, sudden job changes, or misuse in biology. These are the kind of things good preparation already helps with, whatever the cause.

So the first layer of preparation is boring and powerful.

1. Keep some savings and basic supplies for a few days without power or internet.
2. Have paper copies of important numbers and documents.
3. Use strong passwords, a password manager and two factor authentication.
4. Agree with your family on a code word, so a voice clone call cannot fool you.

## The second layer is community

In every crisis, people with strong local ties do better. Know your neighbours. Be part of something offline. Help one older person in your family learn to spot scams. These things matter in every future, good or bad.

## The third layer is your mind

Thinking about extinction risk can hurt. I have had nights where I could not sleep. What helps me is turning the feeling into one action, then letting myself rest. The Prepare page has a breathing pause for a reason.

## The fourth layer is your voice

The biggest preparation is helping the world avoid the worst outcomes in the first place. Understand the issue, talk about it calmly, support sensible rules and organisations that work on safety.
