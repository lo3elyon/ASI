In April 2025, Daniel Kokotajlo and a small team at the AI Futures Project published **AI 2027**, a month by month story of how the next few years could unfold. I have already made a whole explainer about it in this repo, but I want to share what it changed for me personally.

## Concrete beats abstract

Before reading it, "superintelligence" was a fuzzy word. AI 2027 turned it into a sequence. Agents that help with coding. Agents that run most of the research inside a lab. A fast loop where AI improves AI. Governments waking up late. A choice between racing and slowing down.

When you see it as steps, you can ask at each step: would this really happen, and what would stop it?

## The two endings

The scenario has two endings. In one, the race continues and humans lose control. In the other, people slow down at a key moment, invest in safety and keep control. The authors were clear that these are not the only possibilities.

That second ending is the part people forget. The scenario is not "we are doomed in 2027". It is "here is a fork, and here is what the good branch requires".

## What people get wrong

The most common mistake I see is people saying "it is late 2026 and the world does not look exactly like the story, so it was wrong". A scenario is not a prophecy. The authors themselves have updated their timelines over time. The value is in the mechanisms it describes, like race dynamics and automated research, not in the exact month.

## How I use it now

When I read AI news now, I ask which step of the story we are on. That simple habit makes the news easier to understand and harder to panic about.

The Futures page on this site is my own attempt to extend this: six futures instead of two, with sliders so you can feel which levers matter.
