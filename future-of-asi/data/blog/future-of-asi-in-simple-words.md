When I explain ASI to my friends, I start with a simple question. What is the smartest thing on Earth right now?

Humans. Not because we are the strongest or the fastest, but because we can think, plan and build tools. That one advantage is why we run the planet and not tigers.

**Artificial superintelligence** means a machine that is much better than the best humans at almost every kind of thinking. Better at science, better at planning, better at persuading, better at hacking, and better at building the next AI.

## Why it might come fast

The scary and exciting part is that last point. If an AI can do AI research, it can help build a better AI. That one can build a better one again. People have talked about this since I. J. Good wrote about an intelligence explosion in 1965.

Today it does not feel like science fiction anymore. Models write a big share of code inside AI companies. Agents can now work on tasks for hours. Forecasters disagree a lot, but some serious ones think we could see AI that beats humans at AI research within a few years. Others think it is decades away. You can compare them yourself on the timeline tool.

## The big ways it can go

I find it useful to think in a few rough futures.

1. **The Race.** Everyone runs to be first. Safety is always one step behind.
2. **The Slowdown.** Something scares us early, and we choose to slow down and build brakes.
3. **Controlled Takeoff.** Progress stays fast, but labs use AI control so that even an untrustworthy model cannot do serious damage.
4. **Many Hands.** Powerful AI spreads everywhere. Nobody controls it fully.
5. **Catastrophe.** A misaligned system, or a person using one, causes harm we cannot undo.
6. **Flourishing.** We get alignment right, share the benefits, and the future gets much bigger.

None of these is fixed. The Futures page lets you move sliders like race pressure and global coordination to feel how choices push between them. It is a toy model, not a prediction, but it makes one thing clear. The ending depends on decisions people make in the next few years.

## What I want you to take away

ASI is not just a tech story. It is a story about power, trust and time. The question is not only "can we build it?" The question is "will it want what we want, and will we still have a say?"

That is the question this whole site is about.
