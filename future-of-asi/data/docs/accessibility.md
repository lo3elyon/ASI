# Accessibility and privacy

## Keyboard

Every control is a real button, link or input. Press Tab to move, Enter or Space to activate, Escape to close drawers, the player and the palette. Ctrl K or Cmd K or the slash key opens search. In the AI control game, A accepts, U audits and D defers. On the voices wall, the arrow keys move between quotes.

## Motion and contrast

The site follows your system's reduced motion setting, and the Motion switch in settings turns all animation off. There is a high contrast theme, and it is chosen automatically if your system asks for more contrast.

## Screen readers

Pages use landmarks, headings in order, labels on inputs and live regions for changing content. Charts carry text labels.

## Privacy

There are no trackers and no analytics. Your course progress, checklist, watch history, theme and tool settings are stored only in your browser with localStorage. Videos load from YouTube's privacy enhanced domain only when you press play.

## Offline

After the first visit a service worker keeps the app and data so most pages work offline. Videos and thumbnails still need a connection.
