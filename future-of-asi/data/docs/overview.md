# Threshold: The Future of ASI

Threshold is one route of the open source [ASI repo](https://github.com/lo3elyon/ASI). It is a static site that explains the future of artificial superintelligence in simple words, shows what AI safety and alignment researchers are warning about, and gives people practical ways to learn and act.

It is made by Aniruddha Adak and built with Hark. The motto is the same as the rest of the repo: start big, fix later, keep shipping.

## Who it is for

Students, developers, journalists, parents and anyone who keeps hearing that AI could be dangerous and wants to understand why, without hype and without maths.

## Principles

Every quote has a source link. Every video ID was checked against YouTube. Numbers like p(doom) only appear when the person said them. Scenarios and toy models are labelled as such. The copy uses simple words, no em dashes and no exclamation marks.

Press Ctrl K anywhere to search everything.
