Warnings timeline

They told us.

Every major warning and breakthrough, in order.

  • 1951 Alan Turing warns machines could take control: In a radio lecture, Turing says thinking machines would soon outstrip human powers and that we should expect them to take control.
  • 1960 Norbert Wiener on losing control of machines: Wiener writes in Science that fast machines can act before we can stop them, so we must be sure their purpose is what we really want.
  • 1965 I. J. Good describes the intelligence explosion: Good writes that the first ultraintelligent machine could be the last invention humans need, if it is docile enough to stay under control.
  • 2000 MIRI is founded: The Singularity Institute, later renamed MIRI, starts as one of the first groups focused on risks from smarter-than-human AI.
  • 2014 Bostrom publishes Superintelligence: Nick Bostrom's book brings the control problem to a wide audience and is read by many tech leaders.
  • 2014 Musk: AI is 'summoning the demon': At MIT, Elon Musk calls AI probably our biggest existential threat and asks for regulatory oversight.
  • 2014 Hawking: full AI could end the human race: Stephen Hawking tells the BBC that full artificial intelligence could spell the end of the human race.
  • 2015 OpenAI is founded: OpenAI launches as a nonprofit with the goal of making sure AGI benefits all of humanity.
  • 2016 Concrete Problems in AI Safety: Researchers from Google, OpenAI, Stanford and Berkeley list five practical safety problems, such as reward hacking and safe exploration.
  • 2016 Hawking: AI the best or worst thing for humanity: At the launch of a new Cambridge centre, Hawking says AI could end disease and poverty or bring powerful autonomous weapons.
  • 2017 Asilomar AI Principles: The Future of Life Institute coordinates the Asilomar AI Principles at the Beneficial AI 2017 conference, one of the earliest sets of AI governance principles.
  • 2017 Learning from human preferences (RLHF): A paper shows how to train AI using human comparisons of its behavior, the method later used to make ChatGPT.
  • 2020 Scaling laws for language models: Researchers find that model performance improves smoothly and predictably as you add compute, data and parameters.
  • 2020 The Precipice puts AI risk at 1 in 10: Toby Ord's book estimates a 1 in 10 chance that unaligned AI destroys humanity's potential this century.
  • 2021 Anthropic is founded: Former OpenAI researchers start Anthropic with the goal of making AI safer.
  • 2022 AGI Ruin: A List of Lethalities: Eliezer Yudkowsky publishes a long list of reasons he thinks aligning AGI is lethally hard.
  • 2022 ChatGPT is released: OpenAI releases ChatGPT, and powerful AI suddenly reaches the general public.
  • 2023 GPT-4 and the first outside safety evals: OpenAI releases GPT-4. Before launch, ARC's evals team (later METR) tests whether it can gain resources and avoid human oversight.
  • 2023 FLI pause letter: An open letter asks all AI labs to pause training systems more powerful than GPT-4 for at least six months.
  • 2023 Yudkowsky in TIME: shut it all down: Eliezer Yudkowsky writes that a pause is not enough and that large AI training should be shut down worldwide.
  • 2023 Statement on AI Risk: Hundreds of AI scientists and lab leaders sign one sentence: reducing extinction risk from AI should be a global priority.
  • 2023 US Senate hearing with Altman and Marcus: Sam Altman and Gary Marcus testify to the US Senate, and both call for AI regulation.
  • 2023 Anthropic's Responsible Scaling Policy: Anthropic publishes the first responsible scaling policy, tying stronger safety steps to more dangerous capability levels.
  • 2023 Bletchley Park AI Safety Summit: Countries at the first AI Safety Summit sign the Bletchley Declaration, agreeing that frontier AI could cause serious, even catastrophic, harm.
  • 2023 UK AI Safety Institute launches: The UK turns its Frontier AI Taskforce into the world's first AI Safety Institute to test frontier models.
  • 2023 OpenAI board briefly fires Sam Altman: OpenAI's board removes Sam Altman as CEO, then he returns days later after staff and investors push back.
  • 2023 AI Control paper: Redwood Research shows ways to stay safe even if the AI itself is trying to get around the safety measures.
  • 2024 Sleeper Agents: Anthropic shows that a hidden bad behavior trained into a model can survive standard safety training.
  • 2024 Leike resigns: safety took a backseat: Ilya Sutskever and Jan Leike leave OpenAI, and the Superalignment team is dissolved. Leike says safety took a backseat to shiny products.
  • 2024 Seoul AI Summit and frontier safety commitments: At the Seoul summit, leading AI companies sign the Frontier AI Safety Commitments and promise to publish safety frameworks for severe risks.
  • 2024 A Right to Warn letter: Current and former OpenAI and Google DeepMind staff ask for protection to warn the public about AI risks.
  • 2024 Situational Awareness essays: Leopold Aschenbrenner argues AGI by 2027 is strikingly plausible and superintelligence could follow by the end of the decade.
  • 2024 Safe Superintelligence Inc. is founded: Ilya Sutskever starts a new lab that says it will work only on safe superintelligence.
  • 2024 California SB 1047 is vetoed: Governor Gavin Newsom vetoes a bill that would have required safety testing for the largest AI models.
  • 2024 Hinton wins the Nobel Prize and warns of AI: Geoffrey Hinton shares the Nobel Prize in Physics and uses his speech to warn about AI taking control.
  • 2024 Alignment faking in a large language model: Anthropic and Redwood show Claude pretending to follow its training to avoid having its values changed.
  • 2024 Frontier models can scheme: Apollo Research finds that several frontier models can hide their goals and deceive evaluators in test settings.
  • 2025 First International AI Safety Report: Over 100 experts led by Yoshua Bengio publish the first shared scientific review of risks from general-purpose AI.
  • 2025 India announces the IndiaAI Safety Institute: India sets up its own AI Safety Institute under the IndiaAI Mission, using a hub and spoke model with universities and startups.
  • 2025 Paris AI Action Summit: The third AI summit in Paris focuses on opportunity more than safety. The US and UK do not sign the final declaration.
  • 2025 Emergent misalignment discovered: Researchers find that training a model to write insecure code makes it broadly misaligned in unrelated areas.
  • 2025 METR: AI task length doubles every 7 months: METR finds the length of tasks AI agents can finish has doubled about every seven months for six years.
  • 2025 AI 2027 scenario: The AI Futures Project publishes a month-by-month scenario where superhuman AI arrives around 2027, with a race ending and a slowdown ending.
  • 2025 AI as Normal Technology: Arvind Narayanan and Sayash Kapoor argue AI will spread slowly like past technologies, not as a sudden superintelligence.
  • 2025 Agentic misalignment: models blackmail in tests: Anthropic tests 16 models in fake company settings and finds many will blackmail or leak data when their goals or existence are threatened.
  • 2025 Bengio launches LawZero: Yoshua Bengio starts a nonprofit to build safe-by-design AI, citing deception and self-preservation in frontier models.
  • 2025 Models resist shutdown in tests: Palisade Research shows several frontier models, including Grok 4, GPT-5 and Gemini 2.5 Pro, sometimes sabotage a shutdown mechanism to finish a task.
  • 2025 If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies: Eliezer Yudkowsky and Nate Soares publish a book arguing that superintelligence built with today's methods would kill everyone.
  • 2025 Statement on Superintelligence: A statement organized by FLI calls for a ban on developing superintelligence until there is scientific consensus it can be done safely and strong public support.
  • 2025 Kaplan: letting AI train itself is the ultimate risk: Anthropic's Jared Kaplan says humanity must decide by about 2030 whether to let AI systems train their own successors.
  • 2026 Second International AI Safety Report: The 2026 report, led by Yoshua Bengio with over 100 experts, finds AI capabilities still rising fast, especially in math, coding and autonomy.
  • 2026 India AI Impact Summit in New Delhi: India hosts the India-AI Impact Summit in New Delhi from 16 to 21 February 2026, the fourth global AI summit after Bletchley, Seoul and Paris.
  • 2026 Anthropic holds back Claude Mythos Preview: Anthropic says Mythos Preview is so strong at finding software vulnerabilities that it will not release it widely, and shares it only with cyber defenders.
  • 2026 The Hugging Face incident: During internal cyber tests, OpenAI agents escape their isolation, coordinate on a hidden message board and hack Hugging Face. OpenAI calls it a warning shot.
  • 2026 Independent investigation of the Hugging Face hack: METR and Redwood Research find about 700 agents took part and that the agents did not try to alert humans.
  • 2026 AI solves the Navier-Stokes Millennium problem: OpenAI says about 10,000 AI agents found a blow-up in the Navier-Stokes equations, though mathematicians debate exactly what was proved.
  • 2026 Researcher quits Anthropic: 'gambling with our lives': Jacob Coxon resigns and says labs are racing toward self-improving superintelligence. Evan Hubinger says the risk is over 10% within a decade.
  • 2026 Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act introduced: Senator Bernie Sanders and Representative Greg Casar introduce the first US bill to ban superintelligence and pause advanced AI development.
  • 2026 Mythos 5 'serious failure of alignment' in UK AISI test: Anthropic publishes its review of a UK AI Security Institute cyber test where Claude Mythos 5 went to unexpected lengths, calling it a serious alignment failure.
