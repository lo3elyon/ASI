Organisations

Who is keeping watch.

  • Anthropic, frontier lab, USA: An AI company that builds the Claude models and does a lot of public safety research, like interpretability and alignment stress tests.
  • OpenAI (safety work), frontier lab, USA: The company behind ChatGPT. Its safety teams work on preparedness, red teaming and alignment, and several safety leaders have left publicly.
  • Google DeepMind (AGI safety), frontier lab, UK: Google's AI lab. Its AGI safety teams study misuse and misalignment, interpretability and its Frontier Safety Framework.
  • Redwood Research, research nonprofit, USA: A research group that started the field of AI control: staying safe even if a powerful AI is secretly trying to cause harm.
  • METR (Model Evaluation and Threat Research), evaluations nonprofit, USA: Tests frontier AI models for dangerous abilities, like doing long tasks alone, and tracks how fast that ability grows.
  • Apollo Research, evaluations nonprofit, UK: Tests whether AI models can scheme, meaning hide their real goals or deceive the people checking them.
  • Machine Intelligence Research Institute (MIRI), research and advocacy nonprofit, USA: One of the oldest AI risk groups. It now works mainly on public communication and policy to stop the race to superintelligence.
  • Alignment Research Center (ARC), research nonprofit, USA: Works on theory for explaining why neural networks behave the way they do, so we can catch bad behavior before it happens.
  • UK AI Security Institute, government institute, UK: The UK government's team that tests frontier AI models for serious risks and funds safety research. It was the first institute of its kind.
  • US Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI), government institute, USA: The US government center inside NIST that tests and measures AI systems. It began as the US AI Safety Institute and was renamed in 2025.
  • Center for AI Safety (CAIS), research and field-building nonprofit, USA: Does safety research, runs compute for researchers, and organized the 2023 Statement on AI Risk.
  • Future of Life Institute (FLI), advocacy nonprofit, USA: Works to steer powerful technology away from large scale risks. It organized the 2023 pause letter and the 2025 Statement on Superintelligence.
  • Centre for the Governance of AI (GovAI), research nonprofit, UK: Researches how governments and companies should govern advanced AI, and trains new policy researchers.
  • Epoch AI, research nonprofit: Collects data on AI progress, like how much compute and data models use, and makes clear charts about trends.
  • AI Futures Project, forecasting nonprofit, USA: Writes detailed forecasts and scenarios about advanced AI. Best known for the AI 2027 scenario.
  • FAR.AI, research nonprofit, USA: Runs research on robustness and alignment and hosts events that connect safety researchers.
  • Palisade Research, research nonprofit, USA: Studies what today's AI agents can do, such as resisting shutdown or hacking, to show the risk of losing control.
  • Conjecture, AI safety company, UK: A London AI safety company focused on building AI that stays bounded and controllable.
  • Center for Human-Compatible AI (CHAI), university research center, USA: A Berkeley research center working on AI that is provably beneficial and stays uncertain about what humans want.
  • BlueDot Impact, education nonprofit, UK: Runs free online courses on AI safety, AI governance and biosecurity, and helps graduates start careers in the field.
  • MATS (ML Alignment and Theory Scholars), fellowship program, USA: A mentorship program that pairs new researchers with senior AI safety mentors for focused research projects.
  • Constellation, research center, USA: A shared research space in Berkeley that hosts AI safety researchers and runs fellowship programs.
  • LawZero, research nonprofit, Canada: A nonprofit started by Yoshua Bengio to build safe-by-design AI that puts safety ahead of commercial pressure.
  • Safe Superintelligence Inc. (SSI), frontier lab, USA: A lab that says it works only on building safe superintelligence, with no other products.
  • ControlAI, advocacy nonprofit, UK: Campaigns for an international ban on developing superintelligence and briefs lawmakers about extinction risk.
  • Forethought, research nonprofit, UK: Researches how society can get ready for an intelligence explosion, including risks like AI-enabled coups.
  • AI Impacts, research project, USA: Runs the largest surveys of AI researchers about when AI will get very capable and what the risks are.
  • Centre for the Study of Existential Risk (CSER), university research center, UK: A Cambridge University center that studies risks that could threaten humanity, including AI.
  • Center for Humane Technology, advocacy nonprofit, USA: Works to make technology serve people, and warns about racing to deploy AI without safeguards.
  • Center for Security and Emerging Technology (CSET), university think tank, USA: A Georgetown University think tank that studies how AI and other new technologies affect security.
  • Truthful AI, research nonprofit, USA: A Berkeley research group studying honesty, deception and surprising emergent behavior in language models.
  • ARENA (Alignment Research Engineer Accelerator), training program, UK: A hands-on program that teaches the machine learning engineering skills needed for alignment research.
  • 80,000 Hours, careers nonprofit, UK: Gives free career advice for people who want to work on the world's most pressing problems, with AI safety as a top priority.
  • PauseAI, grassroots movement, Netherlands: A volunteer movement that campaigns for a global pause on building more powerful AI until it can be made safe.
  • IndiaAI Safety Institute, government institute, India: India's AI safety institute under the IndiaAI Mission. It uses a hub and spoke model with universities, startups and industry to test AI for risks in India's languages and social context.
  • Cooperative AI Foundation, charity, UK: Funds research on how AI systems can cooperate well with each other and with humans.
  • SaferAI, research nonprofit, France: Rates and compares how well AI companies manage risk, and works on risk management standards.
  • Coefficient Giving (formerly Open Philanthropy), funder, USA: One of the largest funders of AI safety and governance work in the world.
  • European AI Office, government, Belgium: The European Commission's office for AI. It oversees general-purpose AI models under the EU AI Act.
  • Canadian AI Safety Institute, government, Canada: Canada's national AI safety institute, launched in November 2024. Its research program is run with CIFAR.
  • Japan AI Safety Institute (J-AISI), government, Japan: Japan's AI safety institute, set up in February 2024 inside the Information-technology Promotion Agency. It works on safety evaluation methods, red teaming and standards.
  • Korea AI Safety Institute, government, South Korea: South Korea's AI safety institute, opened in November 2024 under ETRI. It evaluates AI risks and works with other safety institutes.
  • Digital Trust Centre (Singapore AI Safety Institute), government, Singapore: The Digital Trust Centre at NTU Singapore, designated as Singapore's AI Safety Institute.
  • INESIA (France), government, France: France's national institute for AI evaluation and security, created in January 2025. It links ANSSI, Inria, LNE and PEReN under the SGDSN and the DGE.
  • Australian AI Safety Institute, government, Australia: Australia's AI safety institute, announced in November 2025 inside the Department of Industry, Science and Resources. It analyses emerging AI capabilities, risks and harms.
  • Concordia AI, nonprofit, China: A Beijing-based social enterprise on AI safety and governance. It does research, convenings, consulting and frontier risk monitoring.
  • Shanghai AI Laboratory, lab, China: A major Chinese AI research lab. It builds the InternLM models and runs safety and open evaluation platforms.
  • Institute for AI International Governance, Tsinghua University, academic, China: Tsinghua University's institute for international AI governance, led by Xue Lan.
  • Mila - Quebec AI Institute, academic, Canada: A large deep learning research institute in Montreal founded by Yoshua Bengio.
  • Vector Institute, academic, Canada: A Toronto AI research institute that links universities and industry.
  • Oxford Martin AI Governance Initiative, academic, United Kingdom: An Oxford University centre for frontier, international and technical AI governance.
  • Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence, academic, United Kingdom: A Cambridge University centre that studies the future of AI, including its risks, ethics and governance.
  • ETH AI Center, academic, Switzerland: ETH Zurich's hub for AI research across departments.
  • Tubingen AI Center and ELLIS Institute Tubingen, academic, Germany: A European AI research hub in Tubingen, linked with the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems and the ELLIS Institute.
  • Goodfire, lab, United States: An AI interpretability research lab and public benefit corporation. It builds tools to understand, monitor and align AI models.
  • Transluce, nonprofit, United States: An independent nonprofit lab for public oversight of AI. It builds open tools to understand AI systems.
  • EleutherAI, nonprofit: A nonprofit research group that builds open models, datasets and evaluation tools, with work on open-weight safety.
  • Simplex, nonprofit, United States: An AI safety research group building a theory of how neural networks organize their internal representations. It hires in the Bay Area and London.
  • Timaeus (now Resolution), nonprofit: An AI safety research group that uses singular learning theory to study how training data shapes AI behavior. It now goes by Resolution.
  • Apart Research, nonprofit: A nonprofit AI safety organization that runs research sprints and fellowships, with a research focus on harmful manipulation by AI.
  • CeSIA (French Center for AI Safety), nonprofit, France: An independent French think tank on preventing major AI risks. It runs the Global Call for AI Red Lines and the AI Safety Atlas.
  • ERA Fellowship, training, United Kingdom: A Cambridge research fellowship on risks from AI.
  • London Initiative for Safe AI (LISA), nonprofit, United Kingdom: An AI safety centre and ecosystem in London that hosts research organizations and individuals.
  • Centre for Long-Term Resilience, nonprofit, United Kingdom: An independent UK think tank on resilience to extreme risks, including AI and biosecurity.
  • Meridian Cambridge, nonprofit, United Kingdom: A Cambridge research hub and launch platform for AI safety research programmes.
  • Institute for AI Policy and Strategy (IAPS), nonprofit: A nonpartisan think tank that writes policy research on risks from powerful AI.
  • The Future Society, nonprofit: An independent nonprofit based in the US and Europe that works to align AI through better governance.
  • Frontier Model Forum, industry: An industry body of frontier AI companies that shares safety and security practice, including on AI and bio, cyber and nuclear risks.
  • Partnership on AI, nonprofit: A nonprofit coalition of companies, academics and civil society on responsible AI, including AI safety.
  • International AI Safety Report, government: A yearly report on the risks of general-purpose AI, chaired by Yoshua Bengio and written by experts from many countries.
  • ILINA Program, nonprofit, Kenya: An African-led organization that works on AI safety through research, policy work and talent development.
  • Global Center on AI Governance, nonprofit, South Africa: An African-led center on AI policy and governance that works to reduce global inequalities made worse by AI. It runs the African Observatory.
  • Centre for Responsible AI (CeRAI), IIT Madras, academic, India: IIT Madras's Centre for Responsible AI, which works on responsible and safe AI research and AI governance.
  • Digital Futures Lab, nonprofit, India: An independent research studio in India on technology and society in the Majority World, working toward safe digital futures.
  • AI Safety Asia, nonprofit: A network that works to give Asian countries a stronger voice in steering frontier AI.
  • Safe AI Forum, nonprofit: A nonprofit that drives global coordination to reduce extreme AI risks. It runs the International Dialogues on AI Safety (IDAIS).
  • Eleos AI Research, nonprofit: A nonprofit that studies the possible wellbeing and moral status of AI systems.
  • Mathematical AI Safety Institute, nonprofit: An independent nonprofit building mathematical foundations for the safety of powerful AI.
  • AI Safety Tokyo, community, Japan: A Tokyo community that runs AI safety study groups.
  • ALTER, nonprofit, Israel: An Israeli nonprofit for research and advocacy on the long-term future, including AI alignment research.
