AI → AGI → ASI, one step at a time

Three levels of machine smarts. Tap a step to open it, and watch the meter climb.

Good at specific tasks. It does one kind of job very well, but can’t easily switch to a new one.

  • Chatbots
  • Image tools
  • Recommendations
  • Coding helpers

About as capable as a skilled human at most mental jobs. It can learn new tasks on its own, without being built for each one.

Much smarter than the best humans at almost everything, and much faster.

Why it matters: whoever builds and controls it could reshape the world. The upside is huge, and so are the risks:

  • Misalignment: it pursues goals we didn’t mean to give it.
  • Concentration of power: a few people end up holding most of the control.
